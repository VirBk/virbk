## The first lane, in brief

one. Added `parseTrapsYaml` (block-list subset), `parseTrapsMarkdown` (col-0
bullets, ATX headings), `trapsReport`, `splitCheck`, `mintId`, `trapsCandidate`,

signals. One function, `splitCheck`, decides "names a check" for both formats: a

id repeated in this file`), reported in `report.files[].held` and printed.

Accepted spellings: `Check is <cmd>` and `Check: <cmd>`, at the start of the text,
after `. ` or `; `, or at a line start with optional indentation. Refused — read
as "names no check", counted and held by id, never emitted: lower-case `check is`,
`CHECK IS`, lower-case `check:`, and `Check` with no sentence boundary before it
(so `no check is a diary` is not a directive). Refusing is the safe way to be
wrong: a held row costs the owner a glance, an emitted row with a junk check
costs the owner trust.
